Privacy Policy

App: Follower_Following_List_App
Effective date: March 9, 2026

Follower_Following_List_App (“the App”) is a personal-use application that helps analyze TikTok follower/following data for the authorized user.

1) Data We Collect

The App may access and/or process the following categories of data, depending on what you authorize and what TikTok’s APIs provide:

  • TikTok account identifiers (e.g., open_id/union_id or similar)
  • Profile information (e.g., display name, avatar URL, username, follower/following counts)
  • Lists of followers and accounts you follow (if available through the authorized TikTok product/scopes)
  • Related metadata necessary to perform analytics (e.g., timestamps, pagination cursors)

2) How We Use Data

The App uses data only to:

  • Retrieve and display your follower and following lists (as available)
  • Generate analytics and comparison reports (e.g., who you follow vs who follows back)
  • Export reports locally (e.g., CSV)

3) Where Data Is Stored

  • All data is stored locally on your device only.
  • The App does not upload your data to any external server controlled by the developer.

4) Data Sharing

  • The App does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • The App does not use data for advertising.

5) Data Retention

  • Data remains on your device until you delete it (e.g., by removing the App’s local database/files).
  • You can delete stored data at any time by deleting the local app folder/database created by the App.

6) Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect locally stored data (e.g., limiting where files are stored, avoiding unnecessary logging). However, no system is 100% secure.

7) Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8) Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

9) Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: mohog25@gmail.com
